If a line is crossed

Consequences work best when they're predictable, related to what happened, and delivered calmly — then followed by repair. Consistency matters more than harshness. A consequence is meant to teach, not to punish.

The one rule above all the others

THE SAFETY OVERRIDE
“If you're ever unsafe, call. You will not be in trouble for calling.”

A young person who fears the consequence more than the danger will stay in the danger. This rule outranks every other one on the page. Anything else gets talked about later — once everyone is safe and calm.
